# 首批 10 词 · 场景对话集

> 配套：[30天口语流利计划-首批10词](30天口语流利计划-首批10词.md)
> 覆盖 10 个词：**get / go / have / do / take / make / come / know / think / want**
> 目标场景：打招呼、自我介绍、点餐、问路、购物、约时间、表达喜好、求助、闲聊

---

## ⚠️ 使用说明（先读这段）

**不要通读，要"演"。** 正确用法：

1. **遮住中文**，只读英文，看能否理解
2. **角色扮演**：一人分饰两角，出声读出 A 和 B
3. **替换练习**：把划线部分换成你自己的真实信息
4. **不看文本复述**：理解后合上文件，用自己的话重说一遍

> 每个对话后都标出了**用到的目标词**，方便你确认覆盖率。

## 场景 1 · 打招呼与自我介绍

**Dialogue 1-A：初次见面**

> **A:** Hi, I'm Li. Nice to meet you.
> **B:** Nice to meet you too. I'm Tom. Where do you **come** from?
> **A:** I **come** from China. And you?
> **B:** I'm from Canada. What do you **do**?
> **A:** I'm a software engineer. What about you?
> **B:** I **work** in a hospital. *(注：work 属下一批词汇，此处先认识)*
> **A:** That's great. I **have** to **go** now, but let's keep in touch.
> **B:** Sure! **Take** care.

**用到的目标词**：come · do · have · go · take

---

**Dialogue 1-B：熟人偶遇**

> **A:** Hey! How's it **going**?
> **B:** Pretty good. How about you?
> **A:** Not bad. I **got** a new job last week.
> **B:** Really? That's awesome. **Do** you like it?
> **A:** Yeah, I **think** it's a good fit.
> **B:** I'm happy for you. Let's **have** lunch sometime.
> **A:** Sure, I'd love to.

**用到的目标词**：go · get · do · think · have

## 场景 2 · 点餐（咖啡店 / 餐厅）

**Dialogue 2-A：咖啡店**

> **Barista:** Hi, what can I **get** for you?
> **You:** I'd like a latte, please.
> **Barista:** Small or large?
> **You:** Large, please. And I **want** a croissant too.
> **Barista:** For here or to **go**?
> **You:** To **go**, please.
> **Barista:** That'll be seven fifty.
> **You:** Here you **go**.
> **Barista:** Thanks. **Have** a nice day!

**用到的目标词**：get · want · go · have

> 💡 **地道点**：`For here or to go?` 是美国点餐必问句（堂食还是外带）。

---

**Dialogue 2-B：餐厅**

> **Waiter:** Are you ready to order?
> **You:** Yes. I **think** I'll **have** the chicken.
> **Waiter:** Good choice. Anything to drink?
> **You:** I'll **take** a glass of water, thanks.
> **Waiter:** Sure. Would you like to **have** dessert?
> **You:** Not today, thanks. Can I **get** the check?
> **Waiter:** Of course, one moment.

**用到的目标词**：think · have · take · get

> 💡 **地道点**：`Can I get the check?`（买单）比 `I want to pay` 自然得多。

## 场景 3 · 问路与交通

**Dialogue 3-A：问路**

> **You:** Excuse me, how do I **get** to the train station?
> **Local:** **Go** straight for two blocks, then turn left.
> **You:** Two blocks, then left. Got it. How long does it **take**?
> **Local:** About ten minutes on foot.
> **You:** Great. **Do** you **know** if there's a bus?
> **Local:** Yes, you can **take** the number 5.
> **You:** Perfect. Thanks a lot!
> **Local:** No problem. **Take** care.

**用到的目标词**：get · go · take · do · know

> 💡 **核心句型**：`How do I get to + 地点?`（我怎么去…）—— 问路万能句。
> 💡 `How long does it take?`（要多久）—— 交通场景必问。

---

**Dialogue 3-B：打车**

> **Driver:** Where to?
> **You:** The airport, please. How long will it **take**?
> **Driver:** About forty minutes with traffic.
> **You:** Okay. **Do** you **take** credit cards?
> **Driver:** Yes, we **do**.
> **You:** Great. Can you **go** a bit faster? My flight is at six.
> **Driver:** I'll **do** my best.

**用到的目标词**：take · do · go

---

## 场景 4 · 购物

**Dialogue 4-A：买衣服**

> **Staff:** Hi, can I help you find anything?
> **You:** Yes, I'm looking for a jacket. Can I **have** a look at that one?
> **Staff:** Sure. What size **do** you **take**?
> **You:** Medium, usually.
> **Staff:** Here you **go**. Want to try it on?
> **You:** Yes, please. … It fits well. I'll **take** it.
> **Staff:** Great. Anything else?
> **You:** No, that's all. Can I **get** a bag?

**用到的目标词**：have · do · take · go · want · get

> 💡 `What size do you take?`（你穿什么码）—— 注意这里用 **take** 而不是 have。

---

**Dialogue 4-B：退换货**

> **You:** Hi, I'd like to return this. I **got** it yesterday.
> **Staff:** Sure. Is there a problem with it?
> **You:** I **think** it's too small. Can I **get** a bigger one?
> **Staff:** Of course. **Do** you have the receipt?
> **You:** Yes, here it is.
> **Staff:** Thanks. I'll **get** you the larger size.

**用到的目标词**：get · think · do

---

## 场景 5 · 约时间

**Dialogue 5-A：约见面**

> **A:** **Do** you **want** to **get** coffee this week?
> **B:** Sure! When?
> **A:** How about Thursday?
> **B:** Let me **think**. Thursday's a bit busy. Can you **make** it Friday?
> **A:** Friday works. What time?
> **B:** Say, three o'clock?
> **A:** Perfect. Where do you **want** to meet?
> **B:** How about the café near your office?

**用到的目标词**：do · want · get · think · make

> 💡 **核心句型**：`Can you make it + 时间?`（你能安排在…吗）—— 约时间高频句。

---

**Dialogue 5-B：改约 / 拒绝**

> **A:** Sorry, something **came** up. Can we move our meeting?
> **B:** Sure, no problem. When's good for you?
> **A:** I **have** to **take** care of something this afternoon.
> **B:** No worries. How about tomorrow morning?
> **A:** That works. Thanks for understanding.
> **B:** Of course. See you then.

**用到的目标词**：come · have · take

> 💡 `Something came up.`（临时有事）—— 非常地道的改约理由。

---

## 场景 6 · 表达喜好与观点

**Dialogue 6-A：聊电影**

> **A:** Did you see that new movie?
> **B:** Not yet. Is it good?
> **A:** I **think** so. I really liked it.
> **B:** What did you like about it?
> **A:** The story. It **made** me cry, actually.
> **B:** Really? Now I **want** to see it.
> **A:** You should. Let me **know** what you **think**.

**用到的目标词**：think · make · want · know

---

**Dialogue 6-B：聊工作**

> **A:** How's your new job **going**?
> **B:** It's **going** well, thanks. I'm learning a lot.
> **A:** That's good. **Do** you like your team?
> **B:** Yeah, they're great. But I **have** a lot of meetings.
> **A:** I **know** what you mean. Me too.
> **B:** I **think** it's **getting** better though.

**用到的目标词**：go · do · have · know · think · get

---

## 场景 7 · 求助与请求

**Dialogue 7-A：请人帮忙**

> **You:** Excuse me, could you **do** me a favor?
> **Stranger:** Sure, what is it?
> **You:** I **want** to **get** to the museum, but I think I'm lost.
> **Stranger:** Oh, it's not far. **Go** down this street.
> **You:** Thanks. **Do** you **know** if it's open today?
> **Stranger:** I **think** so. It usually closes at six.
> **You:** Great, thanks so much.
> **Stranger:** No problem.

**用到的目标词**：do · want · get · go · know · think

---

**Dialogue 7-B：请人重复**

> **You:** Sorry, I didn't **get** that. Could you say it again?
> **Other:** Sure. I said the meeting starts at nine.
> **You:** Got it, thanks. And where **do** we **have** it?
> **Other:** Room 302.
> **You:** Okay. One more thing — **do** I need to bring anything?
> **Other:** Just your laptop.
> **You:** Perfect. Thanks for your help.

**用到的目标词**：get · do · have

> 💡 `I didn't get that.`（我没听清）—— 比 `Please repeat` 自然得多，听力跟不上时的救命句。

---

## 场景 8 · 闲聊（天气 / 周末）

**Dialogue 8-A：聊天气**

> **A:** It's **getting** cold, isn't it?
> **B:** Yeah, winter's **coming**.
> **A:** I need to **get** a warmer coat.
> **B:** Me too. **Do** you prefer winter or summer?
> **A:** I **think** I like autumn best.
> **B:** Same here. The weather's perfect.

**用到的目标词**：get · come · do · think

> 💡 `It's getting cold.`（天变冷了）—— go/get + 形容词是描述变化的核心结构。

---

**Dialogue 8-B：聊周末**

> **A:** What did you **do** over the weekend?
> **B:** I **went** hiking with some friends. How about you?
> **A:** I just stayed home. I **had** a lot of work to **do**.
> **B:** That's too bad. You should **take** a break.
> **A:** I **know**. Maybe next weekend.
> **B:** **Want** to join us? We're **going** to the beach.
> **A:** Sounds great. I'd love to.

**用到的目标词**：do · go · have · take · know · want

---

## 场景 9 · 结束对话与道别

**Dialogue 9-A：礼貌离开**

> **A:** Well, I **have** to **go** now.
> **B:** Already? Okay.
> **A:** Yeah, I **have** a meeting in twenty minutes.
> **B:** No problem. It was great seeing you.
> **A:** You too. Let's **do** this again soon.
> **B:** Definitely. **Take** care!
> **A:** You too. Bye!

**用到的目标词**：have · go · do · take

> 💡 `Let's do this again.`（下次再聚）—— 结束对话的自然收尾。

## 综合演练 · 一段完整的一天

> 用首批 10 词中的 **9 个**，串成一段可连续说 90 秒的独白。
> **练习方式**：照读 → 遮挡 → 用自己的真实情况替换 → 脱稿复述。

> I **get** up at seven. I **have** breakfast and **take** the subway to work.
> I **get** to the office at nine. I **have** a lot of meetings, so the day **goes** fast.
> At noon, I **go** out for lunch with a colleague. We **talk** about the weekend.
> In the afternoon, I **have** to **make** a few calls. Sometimes things **come** up and I **have** to stay late.
> At six, I **go** home. I **know** I should exercise, but I'm tired.
> I **think** about what to **do** tomorrow, then I **take** a shower and **go** to bed.
> On weekends, I **want** to relax. I **get** together with friends, or I just stay home.
> It's a simple life, but I like it.

**覆盖词**：get · have · take · go · make · come · know · think · do · want —— **10/10 全覆盖**

## 高频句型速查

| 句型 | 意思 | 场景 |
|---|---|---|
| **How do I get to** + 地点? | 我怎么去… | 问路 |
| **How long does it take?** | 要多久 | 交通 |
| **Can I get** + 名词? | 能给我…吗 | 点餐/购物 |
| **Can you make it** + 时间? | 你能安排在…吗 | 约时间 |
| **I have to go.** | 我得走了 | 道别 |
| **I didn't get that.** | 我没听清 | 求助 |
| **Something came up.** | 临时有事 | 改约 |
| **Do you want to** + 动词? | 你想…吗 | 邀约 |
| **I think so.** | 我想是的 | 观点 |
| **Let me think.** | 让我想想 | 缓冲 |
| **You know what I mean?** | 你懂我意思吧 | 闲聊 |
| **Here you go.** | 给你 | 递东西 |

## 说明

- 对话中的英文均为**口语化表达**（如 `Can I get the check?`、`I didn't get that.`），优先自然度而非语法教科书式严谨。
- 标注"下一批词汇"的词（如 work、talk）仅作语境补充，不要求本阶段掌握。
- 部分场景（如打车）在实际生活中较少用英语，保留是为了覆盖 `take / do / go` 的典型用法。
